# Intro to Bioinformatics

Welcome to the **Intro to Bioinformatics** course repository! This repository contains tutorials and supporting materials for the course. It is designed to introduce students to essential bioinformatics tools, biological databases, and sequence analysis workflows through hands-on learning.

## 📚 Course Agenda

### 1. Introduction to Bioinformatics
An introduction to the fundamental concepts of bioinformatics, biological data, and the tools used to retrieve, analyze, and interpret biological information.

### 2. Hands-on Workshops

#### Episode 1: Navigating NCBI — Retrieving and Interpreting Gene and Protein Records
- Explore the National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI) platform.
- Retrieve gene and protein sequences from biological databases.
- Understand accession numbers, annotations, and cross-references.
- Interpret gene and protein records.

#### Episode 2: Finding Biomedical Literature Using PubMed and PMC
- Navigate PubMed and PubMed Central (PMC).
- Search for relevant biomedical research articles.
- Use keywords, filters, and advanced search strategies.
- Retrieve and interpret scientific literature to support biological research.



*Happy learning and exploring bioinformatics!* 🧬
